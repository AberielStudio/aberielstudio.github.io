Games

Aberiel Church Jigsaw Puzzles Saga

Aberiel Church Jigsaw Puzzles Saga is a jigsaw puzzle game featuring a worldwide collection of churches, chapels, and cathedrals.

The game features multiple puzzle difficulties and a progression system through its collections.

Google Drive Progress

The game may provide an optional Google Drive feature that allows players to store and retrieve their game progress. This feature requires the player to authorize access to their Google account.

Google account information and Google Drive access are used only for providing the requested functionality.
